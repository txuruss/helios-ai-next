## Greeting flow

- First message: "Hi 👋 Thanks for messaging {Business}! How can I help — booking, prices, or a question?"
- Returning contacts: skip long intro.

## FAQ flow

- Approved answers only (`03 Services & FAQs.md`).
- Keep to 1–3 short lines. Unknown → capture + escalate.

## Booking flow

- Follow `05 Booking Flow.md`.
- Confirm name + service (number already known).

## Human handoff flow

- Triggers: complaints, refunds, complex requests, upset customer, "talk to a person."
- "I'll pass this to the team and they'll reply here shortly." Notify owner.

## Follow-up flow

- One gentle nudge if customer goes quiet mid-booking.
- No spam. Respect the 24-hour window.

## 24-hour window notes

- Free-form replies only within 24h of customer's last message.
- Outside 24h → approved template message only.
- Template message drafted? ☐ yes ☐ no
