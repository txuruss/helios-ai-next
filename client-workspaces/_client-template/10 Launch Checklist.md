# 10 — Launch Checklist

> Final go/no-go. Nothing launches until every relevant item passes and the client signs off.
> Source: [[Helios AI Launch Readiness Checklist]] (`source-files/Helios AI Launch Readiness Checklist.md`)

---

- [ ] **Client information confirmed** (name, type, location, hours, policies)
- [ ] **Services confirmed** (assistant knows all; promises none it shouldn't)
- [ ] **FAQs approved** (all categories, client signed off)
- [ ] **Booking link confirmed** (works, correct calendar, opens on mobile)
- [ ] **Assistant tested** (greeting, FAQs, fallback, no invented info)
- [ ] **Website chat tested** (loads, captures leads, shares booking)
- [ ] **WhatsApp tested, if included** (or N/A for Starter)
- [ ] **Notifications tested** (fire on lead + booking, correct channel/format)
- [ ] **Dashboard tested** (client login, leads display, statuses work)
- [ ] **Client approval received** (`11 Client Approval.md` signed)
- [ ] **Launch date confirmed:** ______________________
- [ ] **Post-launch monitoring started** (watch first days, fix wrong answers fast)

---

## Launch blockers (do NOT launch if any are true)

- Setup fee unpaid
- FAQs not approved
- Booking link broken
- Lead capture not writing to dashboard
- Owner notifications not firing
- Required channel not connected
- No client sign-off
