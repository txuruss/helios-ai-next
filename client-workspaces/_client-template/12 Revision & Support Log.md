## Priority key

- **P1 Critical** — system down / no notifications / booking broken → same business day
- **P2 High** — wrong answers / lead capture issue → within 1 business day
- **P3 Normal** — wording / minor tweaks → 2–3 business days
- **P4 Low / upsell** — new features → scope and quote
