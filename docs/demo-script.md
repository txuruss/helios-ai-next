# Helios AI — Demo Script

> **Duration:** 2–3 minutes  
> **Goal:** Show a local service business owner that Helios AI solves their missed customer problem in under 3 minutes.

---

## Before Recording

Checklist:
- [ ] Open /demo in a private browser tab
- [ ] Run Demo Mode: load Elite Cuts Barbershop data
- [ ] Verify /dashboard loads cleanly
- [ ] Mission Control shows demo KPI cards
- [ ] Inbox shows a sample conversation
- [ ] Widget sandbox is ready at /demo/widget
- [ ] Screen recording software is ready (Loom, OBS, or QuickTime)

## 2-Minute Script

### 1. Landing page (10 seconds)

> "Every local service business loses customers every day because they reply too late."

Show the / landing page. Point to the headline: **"Stop Missing Customers While You're Busy Working."**

> "Helios AI fixes this by installing an AI booking system that replies instantly — through your website and WhatsApp."

---

### 2. Open /demo (15 seconds)

Navigate to /demo.

> "Here's a live demo for Elite Cuts Barbershop. Watch what happens when a customer messages them."

Click **Run Demo Flow**. Walk through each animated step:
1. Customer messages the website
2. Helios AI replies instantly
3. Lead details are captured
4. Booking request is created
5. Owner is notified
6. Mission Control is updated

---

### 3. Try the widget (30 seconds)

Scroll down to the embedded widget on /demo. Type:

- "Do you have openings Saturday?"
- "How much is a haircut?"

> "The AI answers instantly, 24/7 — even when you're with a client."

Show the response. Point out:
- No wait time
- Accurate service information
- Lead captured

---

### 4. Mission Control (20 seconds)

Navigate to /dashboard.

> "The owner sees everything here. New leads, booking requests, AI status."

Show:
- New Leads KPI card
- Bookings card
- Deployment Score card
- AI Review Queue

---

### 5. Inbox handoff (15 seconds)

Navigate to /dashboard/inbox.

> "When a customer needs a real person, the AI pauses and the owner takes over."

Show the AI Paused badge and the Pause/Resume AI button.

---

### 6. Booking confirmation (10 seconds)

Navigate to /dashboard/bookings.

> "Booking requests show here. The owner can confirm or reject with one click."

Show the Confirm and Reject buttons.

---

### 7. Setup and pricing (10 seconds)

Navigate to /dashboard/setup.

> "Setup takes 2–3 days. We handle everything."

Show the setup checklist. Return to the landing page pricing section:
- Starter: $999 + $249/mo
- Booking OS: $2,500 + $499/mo (highlighted)
- Ops Center: $5,000 + $999/mo

---

### 8. Close (10 seconds)

Return to /demo or /.

> "If you're a barbershop, salon, spa, clinic, or repair shop and you're losing customers to slow replies — this is what Helios AI is built for."

> "Book a free demo and we'll show you exactly how it works for your business."

## Common Talking Points for Local Business Owners

**Problem:**
- "How do you handle messages when you're with a client?"
- "Do you ever miss DMs or texts from potential customers?"
- "How long does it take to reply to a booking request?"

**Solution pitch:**
- "Helios AI replies in under 1 second — every time."
- "It handles FAQs, lead capture, booking requests, and owner notifications automatically."
- "You get one dashboard to see everything."

**Objection: 'I'm already using Instagram DMs'**
- "Helios AI works alongside what you already use. It adds instant replies through your website and WhatsApp so nothing falls through the cracks."

**Objection: 'I'm not tech-savvy'**
- "We set it all up for you. You don't need to touch any code. Our setup takes 2–3 days."

**Objection: 'Is it expensive?'**
- "The Booking OS package is $2,500 setup and $499/month. For a barbershop doing 6+ bookings a day, that's covered by 2–3 bookings you currently lose every week."
