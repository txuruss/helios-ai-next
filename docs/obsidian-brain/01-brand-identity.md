# Brand Identity — Helios AI Agency

See also: [[00-source-of-truth]] | [[02-offers-and-pricing]]

---

## Brand name

**Helios AI Agency**

- Short form in UI: **Helios AI**
- Do not abbreviate to "Helios" alone in client-facing copy
- Do not use alternative names

---

## Positioning

Helios AI Agency is a **premium AI automation agency for local service businesses**.

We build practical, revenue-generating AI business systems — not experiments or vague "AI strategies."

Target clients are:
- Local service businesses (trades, clinics, salons, law firms, fitness, hospitality, etc.)
- Business owners who want to automate repetitive operations
- Owners who are currently losing leads or wasting time on admin

Core promise: **Stop missing leads. Fill your calendar. Run smoother.**

---

## Tone of voice

- Confident, not arrogant
- Direct and plain-spoken — no buzzword filler
- Premium but practical
- Technical enough to be credible, simple enough for a business owner to trust
- Never condescending
- Never gimmicky
- No forced enthusiasm ("Amazing!", "Revolutionary!")

### What to avoid in copy
- "Cutting-edge", "next-generation", "game-changing"
- Vague phrases like "leveraging AI" without explanation
- Emoji in professional contexts
- Exclamation marks in body copy
- Robot imagery or clichéd AI aesthetics

## Visual style

### Direction
Premium dark SaaS. Think Bloomberg Terminal meets Notion meets Linear — clean, dense, credible.

### Color palette

| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Background | Deep navy, near-black (#0A0F1E range) |
| Surface | Charcoal, dark glass cards |
| Primary accent | Premium gold (#C9A84C range) |
| AI accent | Electric cyan / blue (#00D4FF range) |
| Text primary | Off-white (#F0F4FF) |
| Text muted | Slate gray |
| Border | Subtle glass borders, low opacity white |
| Error/alert | Red, understated |
| Success | Muted green |

### Typography
- Clean, modern sans-serif
- Strong visual hierarchy: large bold headlines, small sharp labels
- No decorative or script fonts

### Iconography
- Sharp, geometric icons (Lucide or similar)
- No cheap robot illustrations
- No random emoji in UI
- No gradient blobs or cartoonish artwork

### Spacing and layout
- Strong whitespace — do not crowd
- Consistent grid
- Cards with subtle borders or glass effect
- Prominent CTAs with high contrast

---

## UI direction

- Dark mode by default (potentially dark-only)
- Glass card components with backdrop blur
- Subtle animations (Framer Motion — already installed) — entrance only, not looping
- Navigation: minimal, sidebar or top bar depending on context
- Data-dense views (Mission Control, dashboards) should feel like a professional ops tool
- Marketing pages should feel like a premium SaaS landing page, not a service business website

---

## Landing page design principles

1. Lead with a clear outcome — not "We use AI", but "You stop missing bookings"
2. Show the product — screenshots, demos, or embed previews
3. Social proof comes before pricing
4. CTAs are specific: "Book a free audit" not "Get started"
5. One primary CTA per section
6. No stock photos of smiling people in offices
7. No busy hero sections with gradients, blobs, and floating cards all at once
8. Mobile performance matters — keep animations lightweight

---

## What to avoid

- Cheap SaaS templates with colorful gradients everywhere
- Dark patterns in UX
- Fake urgency or scarcity
- Overcomplicating the message
- Adding features that dilute the core value proposition
- Building for edge cases before the main flow works
