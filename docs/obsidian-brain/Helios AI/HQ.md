# Helios AI — HQ

> Top-level hub for the Helios AI business brain. Start here.

## Summary

Helios AI is an AI automation agency that helps **local service businesses stop missing leads and bookings**. We install website AI chat, lead capture, FAQ answering, and an appointment-request flow that notifies the owner — so inquiries that arrive after hours or during busy periods turn into booked appointments instead of lost revenue.

The website is **live** at https://heliosai.agency/ and is *ready enough to begin client outreach*. PayPal and Relevance AI are **not required** before outreach starts.

## Current status

- **Website:** Live — landing page, audit form, and Mission Control admin exist. Not fully finished. `Ready enough for outreach`
- **Audit form:** Submitted successfully locally; production submission not yet confirmed. `Needs testing`
- **Mission Control:** Internal admin system built. `In progress`
- **Relevance AI:** Manual "Run AI Audit" processor built in code, **not connected** (env vars not set). `In progress`
- **PayPal:** Intended provider; no checkout/verification built. `Deferred`
- **Client acquisition:** Ready once the production audit/CTA/contact flow is confirmed. `Ready enough for outreach`

## Primary target clients

Barbershops · Salons · Dentists · Gyms · Home service providers · Restaurants with booking needs · Other local businesses that miss calls, DMs, website inquiries, or appointment requests.

## Key decisions

- Find clients **before** PayPal and Relevance AI are finished. Validate demand, not automation.
- Sell the **outcome** (more bookings, fewer missed inquiries), not "AI automation."
- The first client can be delivered manually if needed.
