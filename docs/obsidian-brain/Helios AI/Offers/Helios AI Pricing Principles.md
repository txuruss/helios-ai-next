# Helios AI Pricing Principles

## Summary

Avoid hourly pricing as the main model. Use **simple project setup fees plus monthly retainers**. Price on business value and the missed-revenue problem — not on how long the build takes. When a client doesn't already know what they want, **sell the audit first**.

## Pricing rule

Do **not** price based only on how long the build takes. Price based on:

- Business value
- The missed-revenue problem
- Complexity
- Number of channels
- Number of locations
- Follow-up requirements
- Dashboard / reporting requirements
- Support level
- Speed of implementation

## The audit as the entry offer

- Some clients know what they want → **quote the build.**
- Most clients don't → **sell the audit first.**

The audit opens the door to: paid setup → monthly retainer → more departments → more locations → more workflows.

## First-client guidance

For the first **$1,000–$1,500** client, sell a **simple** version — Starter or a light Booking OS, **not** Ops Center. Avoid selling full CRM, complex dashboards, multi-agent workflows, multi-location reporting, or advanced analytics; those come later. See [[Helios AI Founder Operating Rules]] and [[Next Steps]].
