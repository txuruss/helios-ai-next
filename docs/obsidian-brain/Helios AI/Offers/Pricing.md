# Pricing

## Summary

Helios AI sells three packages — **Starter**, **Booking OS**, and **Ops Center** — priced on the outcome (more bookings, fewer missed inquiries), not hours.

> Technical/offer-strategy context lives in [[02-offers-and-pricing]]. This note holds the current published price points.

## Current status

`Complete (for current offer)` — current pricing structure below. A separate **first-client offer** ($1,000–$1,500, manual setup) is used to land the first validation client (see [[Next Steps]]).

---

## Starter

- **Setup:** `$999`
- **Monthly:** `$249/mo`

**Best for:** Simple website chat · FAQs · Lead capture

**Includes:**
- Website AI chat
- FAQ answering
- Lead capture form
- Email notification to owner
- Basic dashboard
- 1 revision round

**Goal:** Stop missing inquiries. Reply 24/7.

**CTA:** Get Started

---

## Booking OS

- **Setup:** `$2,500`
- **Monthly:** `$499/mo`

**Best for:** Website chat · WhatsApp · Booking flow · Notifications

**Includes:**
- Website AI chat
- WhatsApp assistant
- FAQ answering
- Lead capture
- Appointment request flow
- Owner notifications
- Basic CRM / dashboard
- Monthly optimization

**Goal:** Turn every conversation into a booked appointment.

**CTA:** Book a Demo

---

## Ops Center

- **Setup:** `$5,000`
- **Monthly:** `$999/mo`

**Best for:** Multi-location businesses · Dashboard · Automation workflows · Reporting

**Includes:**
- Full AI booking system
- Website chat and WhatsApp automation
- Lead dashboard
- Client onboarding flow
- Admin notifications
- Follow-up automation
- Analytics / reporting
- Priority support

**Goal:** Manage every conversation, booking, and alert from one system.

**CTA:** Talk to Sales

---

## Key decisions

- Price on **value**, not hours. Setup fees + monthly retainer is the model.
- Lead with Starter for low-friction closes; Booking OS is the obvious upgrade.
- A discounted **first-client offer** is acceptable to validate demand.
