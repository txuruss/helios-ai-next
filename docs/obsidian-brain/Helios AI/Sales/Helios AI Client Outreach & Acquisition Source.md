**Core goal:** Land Helios AI's first **$1,000–$1,500 client** by contacting local service businesses, offering a free audit or demo, booking discovery calls, and converting interested leads into paid setup clients.

## 2. Core Offer

> Helios AI installs **AI booking and lead-response systems** for local service businesses.

The system helps a business:

- Reply faster to website, WhatsApp, DM, and form inquiries
- Answer FAQs automatically (prices, hours, services, availability)
- Capture customer details
- Send booking links
- Notify the owner instantly
- Reduce missed leads
- Turn more conversations into booked appointments

**Positioning rule:** Do **not** position Helios AI as "just AI software." Position it as a **missed-lead recovery and booking automation system**. The product is the mechanism; the outcome — *fewer missed leads, more booked appointments* — is what we sell.

One-sentence version:
> "Helios AI installs an AI assistant that catches the inquiries you're missing — after hours, during busy periods, on DMs and WhatsApp — answers the common questions, and turns them into booked appointments."

## 4. Lead Qualification Criteria

Score each lead **1–10** across these signals (high = stronger fit):

1. **Likely missed leads** — evidence inquiries slip through (slow IG replies, "DM to book")
2. **Booking / inquiry volume** — enough demand to matter
3. **Social media activity** — active, posting, getting comments/DMs
4. **Website quality** — exists but weak, OR no instant reply/booking
5. **Booking flow quality** — manual, clunky, or "DM us to book"
6. **Response-speed risk** — owner clearly can't reply fast (always working/on-site)
7. **Ability to pay** — established business, real revenue, multiple staff/locations
8. **Owner accessibility** — you can reach the decision-maker directly
9. **Fit for website chat or WhatsApp automation** — their channels match our product
10. **Urgency of the problem** — pain is current and felt, not hypothetical

**Score = average of the signals, rounded.** Categorize:

- **Cold Lead (1–4):** No clear pain, poor fit, low activity, unlikely to pay. → Skip or park.
- **Warm Lead (5–6):** Some clear pain, active online, likely replying manually. → Worth contacting.
- **Hot Lead (7–10):** Clear booking/lead-capture problem, active business, visible demand, likely missing leads, strong product fit. → Prioritize.

> **Rule: Only spend serious time on leads that score 7/10 or higher.** Warm leads get a templated message; cold leads get skipped.

## 5. Best Outreach Angles

Each angle names one specific, believable pain. Lead with the angle that best matches what you actually observed about the business — never stack more than one or two.

- **Missed DM Angle** — "You may be losing bookings when customers DM outside business hours."
- **Slow Reply Angle** — "Customers usually book with whoever replies first. If you're with a client, that reply is going to a competitor."
- **Repeated Questions Angle** — "You shouldn't have to answer prices, hours, services, and availability by hand all day."
- **Booking Chaos Angle** — "Your DMs shouldn't be your booking system."
- **Weekend / Peak Loss Angle** — "Your busiest days are usually when the most leads get missed."
- **Website Gap Angle** — "Your site gets visitors, but there's no instant reply or booking assistant to catch them."
- **WhatsApp Angle** — "A lot of customers want a fast WhatsApp reply before they'll book."
- **Owner Time Angle** — "The system handles the basic inquiries so you can focus on the actual work."

**Angle-selection rule:** Match the angle to the evidence. If their IG says "DM to book," use Booking Chaos. If they're a one-person home-service operator, use Slow Reply + Owner Time. Never use an angle you can't back up from what you saw.

## 7. Outreach Sequence

A clear 10-day cadence per lead. Stop the moment they reply and switch to the reply playbook below.

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| **Day 1** | First DM (A, B, or matched-angle script) |
| **Day 2–3** | Follow-up 1 (offer to just send the demo) |
| **Day 5** | Demo link or soft audit offer |
| **Day 7** | Problem reminder + soft call invite |
| **Day 10** | Final polite close (leave the door open, mark Nurture) |

### When they reply

- **"Send info"** → Send the demo link + ask **one** qualifying question ("Quick one — who handles your DMs/messages right now?").
- **"How much?"** → Give a simple starting point, then move to a call: "Setup starts at $997 with a small monthly after that, depends what you need — easiest if I show you on a quick 15-min call. You free [day]?"
- **"I'm interested"** → Book a **15-minute discovery call** immediately. Send the booking link or propose two times.
- **"Not now"** → Ask permission to follow up later, mark **Nurture**, set a follow-up date.
- **"No"** → Respect it, thank them, move on. Mark **Lost**.

## 10. Discovery Call Script (15 minutes)

Goal: find the bottleneck, show the fix, recommend a package, ask for the close.

1. **Quick intro** — who you are, why this is short and useful (60 sec).
2. **How they handle inquiries now** — "Where do most of your new inquiries come from?"
3. **Where leads come from** — channels, volume, peak times.
4. **What happens when they miss messages** — "What happens when someone messages after hours?"
5. **How bookings are handled now** — "Who replies to DMs, WhatsApp, or website inquiries?"
6. **Identify the bottleneck** — name it back to them in one sentence.
7. **Show how Helios AI solves it** — tie the fix directly to the bottleneck they described.
8. **Recommend a package** — Starter or Booking OS (see §12).
9. **Ask for the close** — "Want me to get this set up for you?"

**Exact questions to use:**
- "Where do most of your new inquiries come from?"
- "Do people usually message you before booking?"
- "Who replies to DMs, WhatsApp, or website inquiries?"
- "What happens when someone messages after hours?"
- "Do you feel like you lose customers from slow replies?"
- "If this system handled those replies and helped people book, would that be useful?"

> The last question is the soft close. A "yes" → move straight to package + price + setup.

## 11. Objection Handling

Rules: keep it short · validate without over-agreeing · redirect to the business outcome · push toward demo or call · **never argue**.

- **"How much does it cost?"** → "Setup starts at $997 with a small monthly, scales up if you want WhatsApp and full booking. Easiest if I show you what fits on a quick call — you free [day]?"
- **"I already have a website."** → "Perfect — this lives on it and catches the visitors who message and don't get an instant reply. Want to see how it'd sit on yours?"
- **"I already have a booking link."** → "Great, we feed straight into it. The gap is the people who ask a question first and never reach the link. That's what this catches."
- **"I already use WhatsApp."** → "Ideal — this answers WhatsApp instantly when you can't, so messages don't sit while you're working. Want a quick demo?"
- **"I don't need AI."** → "Totally fair — forget the AI part. It's really just instant replies and fewer missed bookings. Want to see what that looks like?"
- **"I'm too busy."** → "That's exactly the point — it handles the basic messages so you don't have to. Takes nothing from your side to set up. 15 mins this week?"
- **"Send me more info."** → "Will do — here's a 30-sec demo [link]. Quick one so I send the right thing: who handles your messages right now?"
- **"I need to think about it."** → "Makes sense. What's the part you want to think over — fit or price? I'll keep it short and answer just that."
- **"Can you guarantee bookings?"** → "I won't promise numbers I can't control. What I can promise is every inquiry gets an instant reply and nothing gets missed — that's where the extra bookings come from."
- **"I don't trust AI with customers."** → "Fair — it sticks to your FAQs and hands off to you for anything real. You stay in control. Want to see exactly what it says?"
- **"I don't want something complicated."** → "It's not — I set the whole thing up for you, you just get the notifications. Nothing to learn on your end."

## 13. Free Audit → Paid Offer System

The free audit is the bridge from interest to a paid offer. Audit their public presence and identify:

- Missed-lead risk
- Slow-reply risk
- No booking link
- Weak website CTA
- No WhatsApp automation
- Poor follow-up
- Repeated FAQ burden
- Confusing services
- Weak trust signals

Then convert each finding into an offer using this chain:
**Business problem → revenue impact → Helios AI fix → recommended package → setup fee → monthly fee → next step.**

Hey [Name], here's the quick audit of [Business]:

What it's likely costing you:

- Book calls **immediately** when interest appears

## 17. Claude Usage Rules

For any future Claude / ChatGPT prompt that uses this source.

**When generating outreach:**
- Always personalize by business type
- Keep messages short
- Mention **one** specific business problem
- Use a soft CTA
- Avoid overexplaining
- Do not invent facts about the business
- Do not claim guaranteed revenue
- Do not sound like a mass DM
- Push toward demo or discovery call
