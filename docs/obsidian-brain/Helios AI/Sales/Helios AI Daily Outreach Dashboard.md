## 3. Lead Qualification Quick Score (1–10)

Tick each that's true. **Score = number ticked.** Only spend serious time on **7+**.

- [ ] They take appointments
- [ ] They rely on DMs, WhatsApp, calls, or website inquiries
- [ ] Their booking flow is weak or manual
- [ ] They are active online
- [ ] They likely miss messages
- [ ] They have enough business activity to pay
- [ ] The owner seems reachable
- [ ] Their problem feels urgent / current
- [ ] Strong fit for website chat or WhatsApp automation
- [ ] Visible demand (busy, lots of comments/reviews)

> **Cold (1–4):** skip or park · **Warm (5–6):** templated message · **Hot (7–10):** prioritize.
> Full scoring detail: [[Helios AI Client Outreach & Acquisition Source]] §4.

## 7. Rules

- Do **not** spend more than **5 minutes** researching one lead before first contact.
- Do **not** rewrite scripts endlessly.

- Every interested reply should move toward a **demo or call**.
- **Track everything.**
- **Follow up until the sequence is complete.**
