# Helios AI Discovery Call Framework

## Summary

The first call is **not a pitch — it is to understand.** Find where leads come from, how inquiries are handled, where responses are slow, where bookings are lost, what an appointment is worth, and whether the owner cares enough to pay. Diagnose before prescribing.

## Status

`In progress` — living script. Use after a positive outreach reply ([[Helios AI Cold Email System]]).

## Discovery call goal

Find:

- Where leads come from
- How inquiries are handled
- Where responses are slow
- Where bookings are lost
- How much a booked appointment is worth
- Who handles messages
- What happens when the owner is busy
- What system they currently use
- Whether they care enough to pay

## Call opening

> Thanks for taking the time. The goal of this call is simple. I want to understand how your business currently handles inquiries and bookings, then see if there are obvious places where leads are slipping through. If there is a fit, I can explain what I would recommend. If not, I will tell you directly.

## Questions to ask

1. Where do most new inquiries come from right now?
2. Do people usually message you on WhatsApp, Instagram, your website, or call directly?
3. Who responds to those messages?
4. How fast do inquiries usually get answered?
5. What happens if someone messages after hours?
6. Do you ever miss calls or forget to follow up?
7. How do customers book appointments right now?
8. What is one booked appointment worth on average?
9. How many inquiries do you think you miss or respond to late each week?
10. If we could help you capture even 5 to 10 more bookings per month, would that be valuable?

## Retainer qualification questions

The business model is **setup fee + monthly retainer** (Audit → Setup → Monthly Retainer). These questions reveal whether ongoing monthly work is genuinely needed — and how heavy the retainer should be (light / standard / advanced). Ask them after the core diagnosis questions:

1. How often do your services, prices, hours, or availability change?
2. Who currently updates customers when something changes?
3. How do you track missed inquiries or unbooked leads?
4. Do you want this system monitored and improved monthly, or do you only need a one-time setup?
5. Would monthly reporting on customer inquiries and lead flow be useful?

**Reading the answers:**

- Frequent changes + nobody owns updates → the retainer has obvious monthly work; standard or advanced fit.
- Rare changes, small solo operation → recommend the **light** retainer honestly; do not overpitch.
- "One-time setup only" → respect it. Explain what the retainer covers once, note the system will drift out of date without it, and leave the door open. Never pressure.

## Explaining the retainer (without it feeling like a hidden fee)

Bring the retainer up **before** quoting — it is part of the offer, not a surprise at the end. Use this plainly:

> The setup fee covers building the system. The monthly retainer covers keeping it useful. Each month, we monitor the system, update FAQs, review the lead flow, improve responses, fix issues, and help capture more customer inquiries from your website, WhatsApp, or booking channels.

Then make it concrete for *their* business: "When you change your prices or hours, that's us updating the assistant. When a response confuses customers, that's us fixing it. Each month you get a short report on inquiries and lead flow."

Rules:

- State both numbers together: "$X setup, then $Y/month."
- The retainer is **active monthly work** — monitoring, updates, optimization, reporting — never "maintenance" or "hosting."
- If they ask what happens without it: the system keeps running, but nobody is updating FAQs, reviewing lead flow, or reporting — it slowly drifts out of date. Say it factually, not as a threat.

## What not to do

- Do **not** open with "Here are all our AI services."
- Do **not** overexplain the tech.
- Do **not** talk about tools before the business problem is clear.
- Do **not** present the retainer as an afterthought or hide it until the close.

Diagnosis comes from the answers — recommend the offer ([[Helios AI Pricing Principles]]) only once the bottleneck is clear.
