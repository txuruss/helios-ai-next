# Outreach Strategy

## Summary

Lead with the **pain** (missed leads, slow replies, lost bookings), not "AI automation." Open with a free audit. Keep it short, specific, and human.

## Current status

`Ready enough for outreach` — scripts still need to be written and a 25-business list built (see [[Next Steps]]).

## Positioning

> Helios AI helps local service businesses capture more leads, answer questions instantly, and turn missed inquiries into booked appointments using AI chat, booking workflows, and simple automation systems.

## Outreach angle

- Do **not** pitch "AI automation" too broadly
- Lead with **missed leads, slow replies, and lost bookings**
- Use a **free audit** as the opener
- Make the offer **specific to their business**
- Avoid sounding like spam
- Keep messages **short and direct**

## DM / cold outreach structure

1. Personal opener
2. Specific observation about their business
3. Problem hypothesis
4. Simple value statement
5. Low-pressure CTA

## Example message

> "Hey, I came across your business and noticed you rely heavily on calls/DMs for bookings. A lot of local businesses lose leads when people ask questions after hours or do not get a fast reply. I build simple AI booking systems that answer FAQs, capture leads, and send appointment requests directly to the owner. I can send you a quick free audit showing where you may be losing bookings."

## Key decisions

- The **free audit** is the primary opener and ties directly to the website audit form.
- Start with one or two verticals if outreach feels too broad (see [[Risks]]). Target list: barbershops, salons, dentists, gyms, home services, restaurants with bookings.
