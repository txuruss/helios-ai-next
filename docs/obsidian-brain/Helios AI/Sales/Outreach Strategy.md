# Outreach Strategy

> This script is part of the **Helios AI Client Outreach & Acquisition system**. For full strategy, lead qualification, call scripts, objections, and CRM rules, see [[Helios AI Client Outreach & Acquisition Source]].

## Summary

Lead with the **pain** (missed leads, slow replies, lost bookings), not "AI automation." Open with a free audit. Keep it short, specific, and human.

## Current status

`Ready enough for outreach` — scripts still need to be written and a 25-business list built (see [[Next Steps]]).

## Positioning

> Helios AI helps local service businesses capture more leads, answer questions instantly, and turn missed inquiries into booked appointments using AI chat, booking workflows, and simple automation systems.

## Outreach angle

- Do **not** pitch "AI automation" too broadly
- Lead with **missed leads, slow replies, and lost bookings**
- Use a **free audit** as the opener
- Make the offer **specific to their business**
- Avoid sounding like spam
- Keep messages **short and direct**

## DM / cold outreach structure

1. Personal opener
2. Specific observation about their business
3. Problem hypothesis
4. Simple value statement
5. Low-pressure CTA

## Example message

> "Hey, I came across your business and noticed you rely heavily on calls/DMs for bookings. A lot of local businesses lose leads when people ask questions after hours or do not get a fast reply. I build simple AI booking systems that answer FAQs, capture leads, and send appointment requests directly to the owner. I can send you a quick free audit showing where you may be losing bookings."

## Follow-up sequence

Per lead, over ~10 days. Stop the moment they reply.

- **Day 1:** First DM (matched to their business + the angle you observed).
- **Day 2–3 — Follow-up 1:** "No worries if you're slammed — want me to just send the demo so you can look when you get a sec?"
- **Day 5 — Demo / audit offer:** Send the demo link or offer the free audit.
- **Day 7 — Follow-up 2 (problem reminder + soft call):** "Quick one — is missing messages during busy hours actually a thing for you, or do you have it handled? Either answer's fine. Happy to show you on a quick 15-min call."
- **Day 10 — Final close:** "All good [Name], I'll leave it here. The demo's ready whenever you want it." → mark **Nurture**.

## Demo-link sequence

1. **Tease:** "I've got a 30-sec demo that shows exactly what it'd do for [business type] — want it?"
2. **Send:** "Here's the demo 👉 [link]. Imagine this catching the messages you currently miss."
3. **Bridge to call:** "Want me to walk you through how it'd work for your setup? 15 mins, I'll keep it quick — free [day] or [day]?"

## What to do when they reply

- **"Send info"** → Send the demo link + ask **one** qualifying question ("Who handles your messages right now?").
- **"How much?"** → "Setup starts at **$999** with a small monthly after, scales up if you want WhatsApp + full booking. Easiest if I show you what fits on a quick call — free [day]?"
- **"I'm interested"** → Book a **15-min discovery call** now. Send the link or offer two times.
- **"Not now"** → Ask to follow up later, mark **Nurture**, set a date.
- **"No"** → Respect it, thank them, move on. Mark **Lost**.

## Messages to avoid

- "I hope this message finds you well."
- Anything that overexplains AI or how the tech works.
- Guarantees you can't prove ("you'll double your bookings").
- Long paragraphs / walls of text.
- Obvious copy-paste mass DMs with no personalization.
- Desperate or pushy follow-ups ("just checking in again??").
- Pitching "AI automation" broadly instead of one specific pain.
