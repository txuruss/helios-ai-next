# Helios AI Founder Operating Rules

## Summary

Seven rules to keep Helios AI **sales-driven, not build-first.** If no clients are being contacted, Helios is not moving forward. Outreach is a cycle, research precedes every message, curiosity precedes the pitch, discovery precedes diagnosis, the offer stays simple, and the first client matters more than perfect systems.

## Status

`In progress` — operating rules in force now. Read alongside [[Helios AI Client Acquisition Doctrine]].

## The rules

**Rule 1 — Do not hide behind building.** If no clients are being contacted, Helios is not moving forward. Building more dashboards does not equal business progress.

**Rule 2 — Outreach is a cycle.** Don't judge outreach after one day. Track: leads found, leads qualified, messages sent, replies, positive replies, calls booked, calls completed, offers made, deals closed. (This is what [[Helios AI Daily Outreach Dashboard]] / `/admin/outreach` exists for.)

**Rule 3 — Research before messaging.** No generic messages. Every outreach message needs at least one specific observation.

**Rule 4 — Sell curiosity first.** The goal of the first message is not to sell the system — it's to earn permission to show them a problem.

**Rule 5 — Discovery before diagnosis.** Do not prescribe AI before understanding the business ([[Helios AI Discovery Call Framework]]).

**Rule 6 — Keep the offer simple.** Don't sell "AI automation." Sell: *"We help local businesses stop missing booking inquiries and turn more messages into appointments."*

**Rule 7 — First client matters more than perfect systems.** The priority is not the perfect dashboard. It is: find a lead → start a conversation → book a call → diagnose missed-booking problems → sell a simple setup → deliver manually if needed → improve after real feedback.

## What should change in Helios AI now

**Website messaging** — focus less on "AI" and more on the business pain. Better headline direction (feed into [[Website Improvement Backlog]]):

> Stop missing booking inquiries. Turn website, WhatsApp, and DM messages into booked appointments.
> Helios AI helps local service businesses reply faster, capture leads, and book more appointments without hiring extra staff.

**Outreach positioning** — main outreach offer (feed into [[Outreach Strategy]]):

> I help local service businesses find where booking inquiries are slipping through and show how to fix it with a simple AI lead-response system.

**First-client strategy** — for the first $1,000–$1,500 client, sell a simple version: website AI chat + lead capture + FAQ responses + owner notification + booking request flow. Avoid full CRM, complex dashboards, multi-agent workflows, multi-location reporting, and advanced analytics — those come later. See [[Helios AI Pricing Principles]] and [[Next Steps]].
