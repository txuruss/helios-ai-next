# Risks

## Summary

The main risks to Helios AI right now are **overbuilding before selling** and **pitching "AI" instead of a painful outcome**.

## Current status

`In progress` — active watchlist.

## Risks and warnings

- The **biggest risk is overbuilding before selling**.
- The **second biggest risk is pitching "AI"** instead of a painful business outcome.
- PayPal and Relevance AI are useful, but **not required to validate demand**.
- Local businesses may not care about the dashboard — they care about **more bookings and fewer missed inquiries**.
- The **first sale may require manual delivery**.
- The offer must be simple enough to **explain in under 15 seconds**.
- Do **not** add too many features before getting client feedback.
- **Avoid building for every industry at once.**
- Start with **one or two verticals** if outreach feels too broad.

## Key decisions

- Sell the outcome; defer non-essential automation. (See [[Decision Log]].)
- Narrow the vertical focus if outreach response is weak. (See [[Outreach Strategy]].)

## Action items

- [ ] Resist new features until first client feedback
- [ ] Pick 1–2 starting verticals
- [ ] Keep the offer to a one-sentence pitch
