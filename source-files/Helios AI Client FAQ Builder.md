# Helios AI Client FAQ Builder

Turn raw client FAQs into approved AI assistant answers. The assistant only ever uses answers approved here.

---

## Purpose

Build a clean, approved answer bank so the assistant responds accurately and never guesses. Approved FAQs are the assistant's source of truth.

## How to collect FAQs

- Pull questions from the [[Helios AI Client Intake Form]].
- Ask the owner: "What do customers ask you every day?"
- Review their inbox, DMs, and missed-call notes for recurring questions.
- Aim for 15–25 real questions before launch.

## FAQ formatting rules

- One question, one approved answer.
- Answers short (1–3 sentences), in the client's tone.
- No invented facts — every answer must be confirmed by the client.
- Note when an answer should end with a booking nudge or escalation.

## Required FAQ categories

Cover all of the following before launch:

### Services
What do you offer? Do you do {service}? What's included?

### Pricing
How much is {service}? Are there packages? Any extra fees?

### Booking
How do I book? Can I book online? How far ahead? Same-day?

### Location
Where are you? Parking? Accessible? Nearest landmark?

### Opening hours
What are your hours? Open weekends? Holidays?

### Policies
Cancellation policy? Late arrivals? Rescheduling?

### Payment
What payment methods? Deposit required? Pay now or at visit?

### Preparation before appointment
Anything to do before? What to bring? Arrive early?

### Walk-ins
Do you take walk-ins? Appointment-only?

### Human support
How do I reach a person? Speak to the owner?

## FAQ answer template

```
Q: {customer question}
A: {short approved answer in client tone}
Action: {none / share booking link / escalate to human}
Approved by: {client name + date}
```

## Approved answer rules

- Only answers the client has confirmed go live.
- If the client can't confirm an answer, the assistant escalates instead.
- Update answers when prices, hours, or policies change.
- Never let the assistant improvise on pricing, medical, or policy questions.

## When to recommend booking

- The customer asks about a service, price, or availability.
- The customer shows clear intent ("can I come in?", "do you have space?").
- After answering a service question, nudge: "Want me to grab you a time?"

## When to escalate to human

- Question outside the approved FAQ set
- Complaints, refunds, disputes
- Medical, legal, or sensitive topics
- Custom requests (events, bulk, bridal beyond the standard)
- Upset or frustrated customer
- Explicit request for a person

On escalation: capture the lead, reassure, notify the owner.

## Example FAQ bank for barbershops

```
Q: How much is a haircut?
A: A standard cut is $20 and takes about 30 mins. Want me to book you in?
Action: share booking link

Q: Do you do beard trims?
A: Yes — a beard trim is $12, or $28 with a haircut.
Action: share booking link

Q: Do you take walk-ins?
A: We take walk-ins when there's space, but booking guarantees your spot. Want the link?
Action: share booking link

Q: Where are you located?
A: We're at {address}, with street parking right outside.
Action: none

Q: Are you open Sundays?
A: We're open {Sunday hours}. Want me to find you a time?
Action: share booking link
```

## Final FAQ approval checklist

- [ ] All required categories covered
- [ ] 15+ real questions answered
- [ ] Every answer confirmed by the client
- [ ] Tone matches the client's brand
- [ ] Booking nudges set where appropriate
- [ ] Escalation rules set for sensitive questions
- [ ] No invented prices, hours, or policies
- [ ] Client signed off (FAQ approval request sent)
