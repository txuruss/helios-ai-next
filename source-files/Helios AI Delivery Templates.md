# Helios AI Delivery Templates

Reusable, copy-paste templates for client delivery. Tone: professional, simple, clear. Replace {placeholders} before sending.

---

## New client kickoff message

```
Hi {client name},

Welcome to Helios AI! 🎉 Excited to get your {package} system live.

Here's what happens next:
1. I'll send a short intake form to collect your business info.
2. We build and test your system.
3. You review and approve.
4. We launch.

To start, please complete the intake form I'm sending next. The sooner it's back, the sooner we go live.

Talk soon,
{your name} — Helios AI
```
