# Helios AI Revision & Support Process

How revisions and support are handled after launch. Keeps clients happy while protecting scope.

---

## Purpose

Define what's included after launch, how clients request changes, how fast we respond, and where revisions end and upsells begin. Clear boundaries keep delivery profitable and clients satisfied.

## Revision policy by package

| Package | Included revisions | Support | Optimization |
|---------|-------------------|---------|--------------|
| **Starter** | Minor tweaks in first 14 days (FAQ wording, greeting, notification email) | Email, standard response | None included |
| **Booking OS** | Reasonable adjustments in first 30 days + monthly review tweaks | Email + WhatsApp | Monthly optimization review |
| **Ops Center** | Ongoing adjustments within scope | Priority support | Monthly strategy review |

## Starter revision process

- Client submits changes via the revision request form.
- Minor edits (FAQ wording, greeting, notification recipient) handled within standard response time.
- After 14 days, ongoing changes become a paid optimization or Booking OS upsell.

## Booking OS revision process

- Client submits changes via the revision request form.
- Adjustments to FAQs, booking flow, WhatsApp messages, and notifications handled within 30 days and at the monthly review.
- Structural changes (new channels, new automations) are upsells.

## Ops Center revision process

- Priority handling of in-scope changes.
- Adjustments tracked and reviewed monthly in the strategy review.
- New departments, locations, or workflows are scoped as additional work.

## What counts as a revision

- Fixing a wrong or outdated answer
- Updating prices, hours, or policies
- Adjusting greeting or tone wording
- Changing notification recipients
- Tweaking an existing booking message
- Fixing a bug in delivered functionality

## What does not count as a revision

- Adding a new channel not in the package (e.g. WhatsApp for Starter)
- New automations or follow-up sequences
- Custom dashboards or analytics outside the package
- Additional locations
- New integrations
- A full redesign of the assistant's purpose

These are upsells or new projects.

## Support request intake

- All requests come through one channel (email or a shared form).
- Each request logged with: client, type (bug / change / question), priority, date.
- Acknowledge receipt, then resolve or schedule.

## Bug report format

```
Client: {name}
What happened: {describe}
Where: {website chat / WhatsApp / dashboard / notification}
Expected: {what should happen}
Steps to reproduce: {1, 2, 3}
Screenshot / link: {if available}
Reported: {date/time}
```

## Client change request format

```
Client: {name}
Change requested: {describe}
Reason: {why}
Affected area: {FAQ / booking / notification / tone / other}
Priority: {low / medium / high}
Requested: {date}
```

## Monthly optimization process

For Booking OS and Ops Center:
1. Review metrics (leads captured, bookings, response rate, escalations).
2. Identify wrong/missing answers and common drop-off points.
3. Update FAQs and flows.
4. Apply in-scope tweaks.
5. Send the monthly optimization update.

## Response priority levels

- **P1 — Critical:** system down, no notifications, booking broken. Respond ASAP, same business day.
- **P2 — High:** wrong answers, lead capture issue. Respond within 1 business day.
- **P3 — Normal:** wording tweaks, minor changes. Within 2–3 business days.
- **P4 — Low / upsell:** new features. Scope and quote.

## Escalation rules

- P1 issues escalate to the founder immediately.
- Repeated issues for one client trigger a root-cause review.
- Out-of-scope requests escalate to a scope/upsell conversation, not a silent build.

## Support boundaries

- Support covers the system Helios AI built, within package scope.
- It does not cover the client's other tools, website redesigns, or unrelated tech.
- It does not cover unlimited free changes — included revisions are defined per package.
- Sensitive customer issues are routed back to the client's team, not handled by Helios AI.

## Upsell opportunities

Watch for these signals and use the upsell templates:
- Starter client wants WhatsApp or a booking flow → Booking OS
- Booking OS client wants automation, analytics, or multiple locations → Ops Center
- Client asks for repeated out-of-scope changes → paid optimization retainer
